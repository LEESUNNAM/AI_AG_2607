Brand Story

From B2B pole engineering to a category of one.

A condensed timeline. Sources vary on the exact founding year — see Our Story for the full, staged account rather than a single unverified date.

1988

DAC founded

Jake La founds Dong-A Aluminum (DAC), which grows into the world's leading premium tent-pole OEM (~90% share), supplying The North Face, MSR, Big Agnes, and Hilleberg.

Helinox research summary, 2026-08-20
~2009

Helinox concept seeded

A brand concept for consumer-facing furniture begins inside DAC. Sources vary — some cite 2009, others cite a 2011 business-unit start; see Our Story.

helinox.com Our Story page (2009); other outlets cite 2011
2012

Chair One launches

A sub-1kg camping chair rated to 145kg, packing into a bottle-sized pouch — the founding proof point of the "ultralight outdoor furniture" category.

Confirmed across all sources, 2026-08-20
2013

Independent spin-off

Helinox separates into its own legal entity from DAC. Several Korean business-press sources describe 2013 itself as the founding year.

Daum News, 2024-06-13; Brunch/EQL Store archives

Design Philosophy

Visible engineering, not hidden hardware.

"We don't make what already exists."

Helinox describes itself as a Design-Led brand — if a similar product already exists, development stops. The curved hub-and-joint silhouette that holds the frame together isn't disguised; it is the brand's "Eclipse" logo language, carried across the product line.

Source: Mobiinside brand-philosophy interview coverage; Outdoornews, "Eclipse" logo-language coverage (research summary, 2026-08-20)
