Material Science

TH72M alloy & green anodizing

Base metalAluminum + Cu / Zn / Mg additives
Alloy nameTH72M
FinishGreen anodizing
Hub / jointInjection-molded nylon

DAC, Helinox's parent engineering company, developed TH72M by adding copper, zinc, and magnesium to base aluminum and heat-treating it — the core technique behind "minimum weight, maximum strength." The frame's connecting hubs are separately designed and injection-molded in high-strength nylon, allowing a sturdy, secure assembly without adding metal weight.

Reported, not independently confirmed: some sources state TH72M was co-developed with ALCOA's Pittsburgh technical center over 4+ years. This detail could not be cross-verified against a primary source in the underlying research and is presented here as reported only.

Source: Helinox research summary (DAC/Helinox material science), 2026-08-20

Fabric & Construction

Polyester + high-strength mesh

Seating and bed surfaces combine a durable, soft polyester fabric with high-strength mesh panels, balancing breathability and comfort against the frame's structural loads.
