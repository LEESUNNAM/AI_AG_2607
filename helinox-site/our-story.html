Our Story

From a Seoul aluminum workshop to "At Home, Anywhere."

The full founding narrative — staged across DAC's 1988 origin, Helinox's brand seed, the 2012 Chair One launch, and the 2013 spin-off — with every date-sensitive claim source-tagged.

On the founding year: sources disagree — 2009 (helinox.com Our Story page; helinoxstore.co.kr), 2011 (DAC business-unit start, per Korean press archives), and 2013 (independent spin-off / "founded" per several Korean business outlets, incl. Daum News, 2024-06-13). Rather than pick one, this page presents the staged narrative below. Research summary, 2026-08-20

1988

DAC is founded

Jake La founds Dong-A Aluminum (DAC) in Korea. His mother was one of the early pioneers of the Korea Girl Scouts, and he grew up around campsites — a formative link between his family history and the outdoor industry he would go on to help engineer. Over the next three decades, DAC develops lightweight, high-strength, precision aluminum tent-pole technology and grows into the world's leading premium tent-pole OEM, supplying an estimated ~90% of the premium tent-pole market to brands including The North Face, MSR, Big Agnes, and Hilleberg.

Source: Helinox research summary (DAC company history), 2026-08-20

~2009–2011

A consumer brand is seeded inside DAC

For three decades, DAC's engineering innovations were known mainly through the outdoor brands it supplied — not under its own name. Around this period, a project begins inside DAC to take that pole-engineering knowledge and turn it into consumer-facing furniture, driven by a simple question: could the outdoors be as comfortable as home? This is also the period during which Young Lah — Jake La's son — takes on the design and creative direction of what will become Helinox.

Source: helinox.com Our Story page (2009 reference); Mobiinside brand interview coverage — research summary, 2026-08-20

2012

Chair One launches

Helinox releases the Chair One: a camping chair weighing roughly 850–890g (figures vary slightly by source and model) that packs into a bottle-sized stuff sack, yet supports up to 145kg — enough for an adult to sit comfortably for extended periods. It is, by every account in the research, the concrete proof point that launches the "ultralight outdoor furniture" category. Over the following decade, cumulative Chair One sales reach an estimated 1.2 million units (as of 2025, per Hankyung).

Source: Hankyung, 2025; Helinox research summary, 2026-08-20

2013

Helinox becomes an independent company

Helinox separates from DAC into its own legal entity. Several Korean business-press sources — including a Daum News report dated 2024-06-13 — describe Young Lah as founding Helinox in 2013, which is the source of the founding-year discrepancy noted above. Young Lah becomes CEO, leading both the business and the brand's creative direction; DAC and Helinox remain affiliated companies sharing engineering heritage.

Source: Daum News, 2024-06-13; research summary, 2026-08-20

Founders

Jake La & Young Lah

Jake La — Founder of DAC (1988). Built the aluminum tent-pole engineering heritage that underlies every Helinox product, over roughly three decades of B2B supply to the world's leading tent brands.

Young Lah — Jake La's son; conceived and built Helinox as a consumer-facing brand and now serves as CEO. Described in brand-philosophy coverage as the driving creative force behind Helinox's style, minimalism, and originality principle ("we don't make what already exists"). Also associated with recent dual-class share arrangements taken to retain management control as outside investment increased.

Source: Helinox research summary; Fashionbiz governance coverage, 2026-08-20

Mission

"At Home, Anywhere"

Helinox's slogan and philosophical throughline: taking a company that spent three decades as an unseen B2B parts supplier and turning its engineering into a brand people carry from campsite to city rooftop. The Korean-language official site frames it as "우리는 특별한 경험의 시간을 선사합니다" ("we offer a special time of experience").

Source: Helinox research summary, 2026-08-20
