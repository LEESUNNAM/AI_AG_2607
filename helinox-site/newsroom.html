Press releases & industry context.

Each item below is dated and sourced. Revenue figures carry explicit "figures vary by source" microcopy rather than a single unattributed number.

2026-04 / 2026-05

Helinox Wear wins first-ever Wear-category Red Dot "Best of the Best"

The Eclipse Pack Down Jacket wins "Best of the Best" in the Wear category at the Red Dot Design Award 2026 — the first time a Korean outdoor brand has won in this category. Combined with a four-award result in the Gear category the same year, Korean press frames this as a "Red Dot 5-award" result, bringing Helinox's cumulative Red Dot win count to 18 (as of 2026).

Sources: Hankyung (2026-04-28), Kukinews (2026-05-06), Sports Khan (2026-05-06)

2026-08

Costco "Special Collection" event runs at Costco stores nationwide

Helinox runs a "Special Collection" pop-up/special-sale event across Costco stores in Korea, extending the brand's presence in large-format retail alongside its online distribution (including Musinsa).

Source: Sports Khan, 2026-08-13

2025-08 — Industry context

US aluminum tariff increase (up to 50%) raises export cost pressure

US tariffs on aluminum products have risen as high as 50%, creating cost pressure for Helinox given the US's role as its largest single overseas market (an estimated 30–40% of overseas revenue). Coverage frames the brand as navigating this pressure on the strength of its engineering/design positioning while also pursuing an overseas listing.

Source: Hankyung, 2025-08-20

Revenue trend — figures vary by source

2022–2024 revenue trend and a 2026 re-entry target

2022 revenue is reported as approximately 76.9–87.7 billion KRW depending on source (Hankyung vs. Fashionbiz — likely a consolidated- vs. separate-financial-statement difference). Revenue is reported to have peaked around 92 billion KRW in 2023, then declined roughly 33% year-over-year to about 61.1 billion KRW in 2024, attributed to a cooling camping boom and lower exports to Japan. Management reportedly targets re-entering the 80-billion-KRW-plus range by 2026.

Figures vary by source. Exact 2022 figures differ between Hankyung (approx. 76.9bn KRW) and Fashionbiz (approx. 87.7bn KRW); confirm against DART (Korea's public disclosure system) for a single authoritative number before using in external communications.

Source: Hankyung, 2025-08-20
