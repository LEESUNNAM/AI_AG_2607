Corporate structure

Operating base vs. global holding structure — not a single "headquarters."
Reporting is mixed: Fashionbiz (2023) describes Helinox's headquarters as Hannam-dong, Seoul. Hankyung (2025-08) reports Helinox establishing a global holding company, Helinox Pte. Ltd., in Singapore in May 2023, with some headquarters functions reportedly shifting there. This page treats these as two distinct facts rather than resolving them into one: Korea (Seoul) as the operating headquarters, and Singapore as the global holding structure, established for international growth. Singapore's lower corporate tax rate (17% vs. Korea's 24%) is cited by Hankyung as part of the rationale.

Helinox has also structured region-specific leadership for Korea, the US, and Europe under this global holding framework, and is reportedly weighing an overseas listing (Singapore or the US), targeted around 2027 per Fashionbiz (2024).

Sources: Fashionbiz, 2023 & 2024; Hankyung, 2025-08-20
