## Sites tried but not usable (noted per skill guidance rather than faked)
- **Dribbble** (tags/fintech-app-ui, individual shot pages): WebFetch consistently returned empty content — the site is fully JS-rendered client-side and no HTML/meta content came through. Used WebSearch to confirm relevant shots exist, but could not extract real direct image URLs, so no Dribbble images were saved.
- **Mobbin** (explore/mobile/app-categories/finance): WebFetch returned HTTP 403 Forbidden (bot-blocked). No images extracted.
- **Unsplash** (s/photos/finance-app, mobile-banking, etc.): WebFetch worked and returned real image identifiers, but results were generic "person holding phone" stock photography rather than actual app UI screens, and several top results were Unsplash+ (paid/premium) photos rather than free ones. Since the ask was specifically for app *UI* reference (not lifestyle stock photography), these were skipped in favor of the Behance UI case studies, which more directly match "핀테크 앱 UI 레퍼런스."
